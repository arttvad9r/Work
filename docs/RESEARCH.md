# Product research

Research snapshot: 20 August 2026, updated by product decisions on 21 August 2026.

## Primary reference

The reference timesheet app validates a calendar-first model, direct day entry and a compact monthly result. Its dense legacy styling and report controls are not copied.

## Adopted patterns

- month calendar as the primary workspace;
- one day-level editor;
- monthly work-day, hour and income summary;
- optional bonus/penalty adjustments;
- a secondary detailed report disclosed by a bottom handle.

## Rejected patterns

- dated table density;
- advertisements in the core flow;
- report/export buttons in the monthly sheet;
- project/task mental models;
- account requirement;
- currency configuration and exchange-rate explanations;
- notes and quick-duration presets;
- large settings screens.

## Positioning

WorkTime should feel like a modern, calm salary calendar. It wins by making one month and one day understandable at a glance, not by accumulating payroll features.
